Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how Jenovate collects, uses, protects, and manages learner, parent, mentor, and visitor information.

Privacy Policy

1 Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to answer enquiries, manage enrolments, deliver learning access, and support students through our programs.

ID

Contact Details

Name, phone number, email address, city, college, and other details shared through forms, calls, messages, or enrolment conversations.

ED

Learning Details

Course interests, batch choices, project progress, support requests, certificates, attendance, and communication preferences.

PAY

Payment References

Transaction status, receipt details, instalment information, and payment identifiers required for accounting and support.

WEB

Website Usage

Basic device, browser, page, and interaction information that helps us improve site performance and learner experience.

2 How We Use Your Information

Jenovate uses information to run its education services clearly, securely, and responsibly.

  • Respond to course enquiries, counselling requests, and enrolment forms.
  • Provide classes, mentor support, projects, certificates, and student updates.
  • Process payments, receipts, refunds, reschedules, and support tickets.
  • Improve our website, curriculum, communication, and learning experience.

3 Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Limited details may be shared only when needed to operate Jenovate services.

Trusted Service Providers

Payment processors, communication tools, hosting providers, and analytics services may process limited information for business operations.

Mentors and Program Teams

Mentors and coordinators may receive student details required for batches, progress tracking, doubt support, or certification.

4 Data Protection

We use reasonable technical and operational safeguards to protect learner information. No online system is completely risk-free, so students should avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, or highly sensitive credentials through public channels.
