From Vision to Impact Shantanu Hadge's Journey

Meet Shantanu — the founder and driving force behind Jenovate. Fueled by a deep curiosity for learning, growth, and human potential, Shantanu set out with a simple yet powerful vision: to reshape how students prepare for the real world. His journey has always been rooted in one belief , education should empower, not overwhelm.

With a strong foundation in understanding student psychology, skill gaps, and evolving industry demands, Shantanu recognized that traditional learning models often fall short. Through hands-on experience working with students, mentors, and institutions, he realized that true impact lies not just in teaching concepts, but in building confidence, clarity, and capability.

This realization led to the birth of Jenovate an upskilling platform designed with purpose at its core. Built alongside a team of passionate educators, creators, and innovators, Jenovate bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world skills through practical, application-driven experiences.

For Shantanu, meaningful education is human-centered, future-focused, and deeply transformative. His work reflects a commitment to helping students grow not just into skilled professionals, but into thoughtful, adaptable individuals ready to navigate an ever-changing world.

“In a world that is constantly changing, students don’t just need information , they need direction. Jenovate was built as an upskilling platform to help learners find their path, develop meaningful skills, and grow into the individuals they are meant to become.”

Shantanu Hadge

Founder & CEO
